1. Who we are

PixelFit Quest (“PixelFit”, “the app”) is an Android fitness / gym logging app published by Tast Studio (Lukas Tast), based in Denmark. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use package com.pixelfitquest.

2. Short summary

  • PixelFit is offline-first. Workout logs, progression, and most settings live in a local Room database on your phone (source of truth).
  • You can use core workout features without creating an account.
  • Google Sign-In is optional.
  • Health Connect access is optional and user-granted. Health metrics read via Health Connect are used on-device for goals and bonuses; we do not sell your data.
  • Cloud backup / multi-device sync / leaderboards are planned as PixelFit Pro features and are not enabled in the current free build. TODO: re-check when Pro ships.
  • You can export your local data (JSON/CSV) yourself and share it with apps you choose.

3. Information we process

3.1 Data stored only on your device (default)

Depending on how you use the app, PixelFit may store locally:

  • Workout sessions, sets, templates, and related gym log data
  • In-app progression (level, XP, streaks, coins, cosmetics unlocks)
  • Body / profile metrics you enter (e.g. height)
  • App preferences (e.g. volume)
  • Sensor-derived workout signals from the device accelerometer / optional gyroscope (IMU tracking) — processed on device for the active workout
  • Local exports you generate in cache before you share them

This local data stays on your device unless you export/share it, back up the device with your own tools, or (TODO: Pro) enable paid cloud sync in a future version.

3.2 Optional Google account (Firebase Authentication)

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google and Firebase Authentication process identifiers such as Google account email, display name, profile photo URL (if provided), and a Firebase user ID.

Purpose: optional account linking / future Pro sync identity — not required to start workouts.
